# Hiring-manager brief (HYPOTHETICAL)

Everything in this file is invented for the demo. The organisation, the role and the candidates do not exist.

**Organisation:** a 12-person AI governance nonprofit in London. Remote-friendly, most of the team in the UK.

**Role:** Operations Lead, full-time. First dedicated operations hire; reports to the executive director.

**What the role owns, in the hiring manager's words (from a 25-minute call):**

1. Finance: bookkeeping in Xero, monthly payroll, expense approvals, the annual audit pack. "Someone who has actually run payroll, not someone who has watched it."
2. People: contracts, benefits enrolment, leave tracking, onboarding. A 12-person org, so this is a few hours a week, but it has to be right.
3. The annual team retreat (four days, 12 to 15 people) and two smaller in-person meetups.
4. Automating the recurring work with Airtable, Zapier or an LLM tool. "We are drowning in small repeated tasks."

**Must-haves:** has owned bookkeeping and payroll end to end; has owned HR systems (benefits, leave) end to end; based in the UK or willing to relocate (UK visa sponsorship available); 2 to 6 years of relevant experience.

**Nice-to-haves:** has built automations with Airtable, Zapier or an LLM tool; has organised a multi-day offsite; interest in AI safety or governance; experience at an organisation under 30 people.

**Traits named:** organised, detail-oriented, reliable, calm under a deadline.

**Location and terms:** London preferred, remote from the UK acceptable. Sponsorship available. Salary band shared separately.

**What the hiring manager does not want:** a project manager who has never touched the books; a first hire who needs a manager for the finance side.
